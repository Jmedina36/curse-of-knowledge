# Battle SFX

Drop these free MP3 files here to enable real sword/combat sounds.
Get them free (no attribution needed) from:
  - mixkit.co/free-sound-effects/sword/
  - pixabay.com/sound-effects (search: sword, impact, hit)

Files needed:
  sword-clash.mp3   — metal sword clang (plays when enemy takes damage)
  sword-swing.mp3   — sword whoosh/slash (special attacks)
  player-hurt.mp3   — heavy impact/grunt (plays when hero takes damage)
  crit-impact.mp3   — powerful heavy hit (plays on critical hit)
  power-strike.mp3  — big impact + whoosh (charged strike)
